## Design Context

### Users
The primary user is an individual using Xiaowoniu as a long-term personal life-management workspace. The fitness module supports frequent, low-friction workout check-ins, weight tracking, goal review, and quick trend scanning on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Focused, calm, and personal. The interface should feel like a mature health journal: encouraging without gamification noise, factual without feeling clinical, and warm without becoming decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light, editorial utility style with strong information hierarchy, restrained surfaces, and compact operational controls. Fitness uses balanced green and coral accents alongside neutral ink and paper tones. Avoid marketing composition, excessive cards, blue-purple gradients, glass effects, oversized headings, and ornamental wellness imagery.

### Design Principles
1. Make daily actions immediate: check in, record weight, and update a goal should stay within one clear interaction.
2. Let data carry the interface: charts, dates, progress, and history take priority over decoration.
3. Keep hierarchy flat: use full-width sections and dividers; reserve cards for repeated records or genuinely framed tools.
4. Design for repeated use on mobile and desktop with stable dimensions, visible focus, and at least 44px touch targets.
5. Meet WCAG AA, respect reduced motion, and never communicate status through color alone.
